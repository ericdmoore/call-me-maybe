When you need to restore

  1. Sign in on the replacement box with doorman login, selecting the house shown below.
  2. Run doorman recovery authorize and approve access with your account passkey.
  3. Run doorman recovery restore --root /your/inspection/folder and enter this phrase privately.

The restore prepares a replacement sheet. Save it, then run doorman recovery confirm with that new sheet's phrase. Files are restored for inspection; services are not started.
