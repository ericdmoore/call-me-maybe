## The problem

Picking up a phone to find someone already talking on it is a small daily
friction that no mobile ever solved — because on a mobile there is nothing to
pick up.

On a house system with shared lines, it is worse: you can genuinely interrupt a
call by lifting a handset.

## What busy lamp field does

Each handset can watch other handsets. A lamp beside the button tells you
whether that phone is idle, ringing, or in a call — before you touch anything.

## Why it is quietly excellent

It turns the phone into an ambient presence display for the house. You glance at
the kitchen handset and know the office is busy, without an app, an account, or
anyone being tracked.

It is also the feature that most makes a house full of SIP phones feel like one
system rather than several devices that happen to ring together.

## Details worth knowing

**Hints are generated for you.** `doorman render` writes the dialplan hints from
`handsets.toml`, so the lamps cannot drift out of sync with the inventory.

**It needs a phone with buttons.** This is one of the few places where a desk
phone genuinely beats a cordless handset — the lamps have to be somewhere you
can see them.
